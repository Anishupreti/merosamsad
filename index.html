Sansad Record

A citizen-run record of Nepal's Federal Parliament — members, bills, committees, and what's changed.

What this is

Sansad Record pulls directly from the Federal Parliament Secretariat's own published pages — members, bill status, and committee assignments — and keeps a running log of what changes and when. Nothing here is editorial; every figure links back to its source.

Data refreshes automatically every few hours. Last refresh time is shown per section below.
